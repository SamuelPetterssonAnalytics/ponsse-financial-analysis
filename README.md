# Ponsse Plc Financial Analysis 2021–2025

## Objective
A five year long analysis of Ponsse Plc, focusing on revenue development, operating profitablity, cash generation, working capital and financial leverage. 

## Deliverables

- [Excel workbook](deliverables/ponsse_financial_analysis.xlsx): source data, calculations, charts and findings.
- [Financial analysis PDF](deliverables/ponsse_financial_analysis.pdf): concise written analysis of the main developments over the period.

## Methodology
Financial figures were transcribed from Ponsse’s published annual reports. Calculated metrics use Excel formulas rather than hard-coded results. Continuing-operations figures were used where appropriate following the discontinuation of the Russian operations, and relevant historical corrections were documented.

## Key findings

- Revenue grew through 2023 while EBIT margins compressed.
- Working-capital movements caused substantial volatility in operating cash flow and net debt.
- Profitability partially recovered in 2025, but inventories and receivables again absorbed cash.

## Sources

Primary financial data and management commentary were obtained from Ponsse Plc's published annual reports for 2021–2025.

[Ponsse Annual Report](https://www.ponsse.com/company/investors/financial-information/annual-reports)

Detailed report, statement and page references are documented in the workbook Sources sheet. Management commentary used to interpret the results was taken from the relevant
annual reports, including the Board of Directors' reports and CEO reviews.

## Scope

This is a historical financial analysis only. Forecasting, valuation, peer comparison and detailed capital-efficiency analysis are outside the scope of Version 1 and may be developed as separate follow-up projects.
